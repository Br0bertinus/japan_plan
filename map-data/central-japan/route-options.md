# Shirahone vs. Okuhida: follow-up route research

This background package supports later comparison if the group selects Lake Nojiri, Shirahone, or Okuhida in the ordered route builder. It does not establish Lake Nojiri as the origin, require an onsen stay, or define the active website itinerary. The ranges are for **May / June 2027** planning, built from currently published 2026 service patterns and real rail/road geometry. Exact 2027 timetables are not available yet. Recheck trains and reserve mountain buses 30-60 days before travel.

## Decision summary

| Question | Shirahone Onsen | Okuhida Onsen-go / Hirayu |
|---|---|---|
| Simplest without a car | More transfers and a narrow final-bus window | Better: direct scheduled bus from Matsumoto to Hirayu |
| Fastest with a car at the cabin | About 2 hr 30 min-3 hr 15 min | About 2 hr 40 min-3 hr 20 min |
| Best Alps pairing | Norikura and east-side Kamikochi access | Hirayu/Kamikochi gateway and Shin-Hotaka Ropeway |
| Better if finishing in Tokyo | Slight geographic and rail advantage via Matsumoto; still a long transfer | Direct Hirayu-Shinjuku highway bus is operationally simple |
| Better if finishing in Kyoto | Viable via Matsumoto and Nagoya | Better progression via Takayama and Nagoya |
| Main constraint | Few scheduled services into/out of Shirahone | Okuhida is a region; the exact village or ryokan affects local transfer time |

**Conditional conclusion:** if the route includes Lake Nojiri followed by an onsen, Okuhida/Hirayu is easier without a rental car. With a rental car already available, inbound times are close enough that the Alps experience and final-city choice should drive the decision.

## Lake Nojiri to Shirahone

### Scheduled public transport: approximately 4 hr-5 hr

1. Taxi from the cabin to **Kurohime Station**: allow 10-20 minutes.
2. Shinano Railway Kita-Shinano Line, **Kurohime to Nagano**: normally 35-40 minutes.
3. JR Limited Express Shinano, **Nagano to Matsumoto**: approximately 50-55 minutes.
4. Alpico train/bus connection, **Matsumoto to Shin-Shimashima, Sawando, and Shirahone**: approximately 1 hr 55 min-2 hr 5 min on current green-season schedules.

This route has the most transfer risk. Current Shirahone schedules offer only a few useful through connections per day, so an early start and reserved mountain-bus seats are important.

### Taxi + rail + taxi: approximately 3 hr 15 min-4 hr

1. Prebooked taxi from the cabin to **Nagano Station**: allow 40-50 minutes; the road baseline is about 37 minutes.
2. Limited Express Shinano to **Matsumoto**: approximately 50-55 minutes.
3. Prebooked taxi from Matsumoto to **Shirahone**: allow approximately 75-90 minutes in mountain conditions.

This is the lowest-transfer option without driving yourselves, but two long taxi legs make it expensive. A full-distance taxi from Lake Nojiri would take roughly the same time as driving, but availability and cross-prefecture pricing should be confirmed directly.

### Rental car or full-distance taxi: approximately 2 hr 30 min-3 hr 15 min

The routed road baseline is **139 km / 2 hr 12 min** without stops or traffic. The planning range adds mountain-road, rest, and navigation margin.

If renting, the easiest sequence is to collect the vehicle at **Nagano Station before going to the cabin**, keep it through the Nojiri stay, and return it at Matsumoto after Shirahone. Picking up a rental only after leaving the cabin adds roughly 60-90 minutes for the trip to Nagano and the rental counter.

## Lake Nojiri to Okuhida / Hirayu

### Scheduled public transport: approximately 3 hr 35 min-4 hr 30 min

1. Taxi from the cabin to **Kurohime Station**: allow 10-20 minutes.
2. Kita-Shinano Line to **Nagano**: normally 35-40 minutes.
3. Limited Express Shinano to **Matsumoto**: approximately 50-55 minutes.
4. Direct Alpico/Nohi bus, **Matsumoto to Hirayu Onsen**: approximately 1 hr 28 min.

This is the strongest no-car option because the last leg is one direct reserved bus rather than the multi-stage Shirahone connection.

### Taxi + rail + taxi: approximately 2 hr 45 min-3 hr 30 min

1. Taxi from the cabin to Nagano Station: allow 40-50 minutes.
2. Limited Express Shinano to Matsumoto: approximately 50-55 minutes.
3. Prebooked taxi from Matsumoto to Hirayu: allow approximately 50-70 minutes.

### Rental car or full-distance taxi: approximately 2 hr 40 min-3 hr 20 min

The routed road baseline is **148 km / 2 hr 18 min** without stops or traffic. The longer distance is offset by a relatively direct Route 158 approach.

For a Kyoto finish, a one-way rental can continue to Takayama or Nagoya, subject to the rental company's permitted drop locations and fee. For a Tokyo finish, returning the vehicle in Matsumoto and taking the Azusa avoids Tokyo driving.

## Onward travel after the onsen

| From | To Tokyo | To Kyoto |
|---|---|---|
| Shirahone | **4 hr 45 min-5 hr 30 min**: bus/train to Matsumoto, then Limited Express Azusa to Shinjuku | **5 hr 15 min-6 hr 15 min**: Matsumoto to Nagoya on the Shinano, then Tokaido Shinkansen |
| Hirayu / Okuhida | **4 hr 45 min-5 hr 30 min**: direct Hirayu-Shinjuku highway bus, or bus to Matsumoto plus Azusa | **4 hr 45 min-5 hr 45 min** by bus to Takayama, Limited Express Hida to Nagoya, then Shinkansen; direct Takayama-Kyoto bus is usually slower once the Hirayu transfer is included |

Road baselines before stops and traffic are about **4 hr 1 min Shirahone-Tokyo**, **4 hr 24 min Shirahone-Kyoto**, **4 hr 7 min Hirayu-Tokyo**, and **4 hr 12 min Hirayu-Kyoto**. Use approximately 4 hr 30 min-5 hr 45 min for realistic rental-car planning.

## Alps-day implication

Both onsens are next to Chubu-Sangaku National Park. Moving the desired Alps experience from a day trip out of Lake Nojiri to the onsen stay removes a major backtrack:

- **Shirahone:** strongest for Norikura, Sawando, and the east-side Kamikochi approach.
- **Hirayu/Okuhida:** strongest for the Hirayu Kamikochi shuttle and Shin-Hotaka Ropeway.

The next useful decision is therefore not just “which onsen?” but **which Alps experience does Jules mean?**
